Embedded Toolkit Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026 · Package ID: com.blessingq.ymodem_uploader · Developer: JAEYOUNG LEE (blessingq)

Summary: Embedded Toolkit has no sign-up and the developer runs no servers. Firmware files and communication data stay on your device (and on a NAS server you set up yourself). Error reports and usage statistics are sent only if you allow them, and problem reports and feedback emails only when you send them yourself. The app shows Google AdMob ads to stay free.

This privacy policy explains how the app 'Embedded Toolkit' (formerly YModem Uploader; Android package ID com.blessingq.ymodem_uploader; the 'app') provided by JAEYOUNG LEE (blessingq) (the 'developer') handles personal information, in line with the Korean Personal Information Protection Act, Google Play policies and the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Why information is processed

The app needs no account or sign-in, and the developer runs no servers or databases. Only the minimum information is processed, for these purposes only. If a purpose changes, you will be told in advance and asked for consent where required.

  • App features (on your device): talking to devices over USB serial and ST-LINK and writing or reading firmware all happen on your device. The firmware files you choose, communication data, logs, operation records, work profiles and settings are stored on your device and are never sent to the developer.
  • Improving stability (optional): only if you allow it, error reports are sent through Google Firebase Crashlytics and app-open statistics through Google Analytics for Firebase.
  • Problem reports, suggestions and questions: used to review and answer reports and emails you send yourself.
  • Ads: Google AdMob ads keep the app free; ad data is also used for ad measurement and fraud prevention.

2. Information processed and how it is collected

The developer does not collect your name, phone number, accounts, precise location, contacts, photos or the content of firmware files. In detail:

  • Received by the developer: only feedback emails you send (your email address, the message, and the app version, OS and device model included in the message).
  • Stored only on your device: firmware file names and paths, communication logs, operation records (CSV), work profiles, terminal macros and app settings. The NAS server address and account are kept in the app settings, and the password only in the operating system's secure storage (Android Keystore).
  • Sent to a NAS (WebDAV) server you set up: only if you configure a 'NAS server' and choose to upload (or turn on automatic upload), backup files, logs, operation records and work profiles are sent over HTTPS to your own or your organization's server. The developer has no access to that server.
  • Error reports (optional): the error and where it happened (stack trace), app version, device model, OS version, device state such as memory and storage, the time, and random installation identifiers (Crashlytics installation ID, Firebase installation ID). File paths and server addresses in error messages are removed before sending; firmware files, communication data and app logs are never sent.
  • App-open statistics (optional): the app-open event from Google Analytics for Firebase with device model, OS version, app version, language, country estimated from the IP address, and an app instance ID. The advertising ID is not used for Analytics.
  • Problem reports (only when you send one): the description you write and, if you choose, the app log of that screen (which may contain communication data). File names and paths, server addresses and account-like text are removed before sending, and you can preview what will be sent. Reports are sent the same way as error reports (Crashlytics).
  • Feedback emails: the app only opens your mail app with the address, subject and message filled in; it sends nothing itself. The developer receives the email only if you send it from your mail app.
  • Ads (collected automatically by the Google AdMob SDK): advertising ID, device information (model, OS version, screen size, language and country settings), IP address (approximate location), ad interactions such as impressions and clicks, and ad SDK diagnostics.
  • How it is collected: automatically while you use the app (error reports, usage statistics, ads) or when you send something yourself (problem reports, feedback emails).

3. How long information is kept

  • Error reports and problem reports: kept by Firebase for 90 days, then deleted automatically.
  • App-open statistics: kept for up to 14 months under the Google Analytics data retention setting, then deleted.
  • Feedback emails: kept for 1 year after the reply so related questions can be answered, then deleted.
  • Ad data: under Google's retention policies.
  • Information on your device: until you delete it or uninstall the app.

4. Sharing, processors and international transfers

The developer does not sell personal information or share it with third parties, except where required by law. The Google services below process information on the developer's behalf (error reports, statistics) or as an ad network (ads), and data is transferred to Google servers in the United States and other countries. See policies.google.com/privacy and policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites for how Google uses data.

5. App permissions

  • USB device access: to talk to the serial adapters and ST-LINK probes you connect. The system asks for permission for each device.
  • Internet and network state: for NAS server connections, error reports, usage statistics and ads.
  • Notifications (Android 13 and later, optional): only for operation-complete alerts.
  • Advertising ID: for showing ads (AdMob).
  • The app does not ask for storage, location, camera, microphone or contacts permissions. It reads and writes only the files you pick with the system file picker.

6. Deletion

  • Information on your device is deleted when you clear it in the app, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app.
  • Turning off 'Send error reports automatically' immediately deletes any unsent reports left on the device.
  • Error reports and usage statistics sent to Google are deleted automatically after the periods in section 3.
  • Feedback emails are permanently deleted from the mailbox after the retention period.

7. Your rights and choices

  • You can turn error reports and usage statistics on or off at any time in Settings > About > 'Send error reports automatically'.
  • Where consent is required (such as the EEA and UK), a Google consent form appears when the app first starts, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings > About > 'Ad privacy options'.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.
  • To access, correct or delete your information, restrict processing or withdraw consent, email ejok9194@gmail.com. The developer responds without delay (within 10 days). Error reports and statistics are stored under random identifiers not linked to your name, so please include the problem report number (starting with R-) or the approximate time so they can be found and deleted.

8. Children

The app is a tool for developing and maintaining equipment. It is not directed to children under 14 (under 13 in the United States), and the developer does not knowingly collect children's personal information.

9. Security

  • All transfers are encrypted with HTTPS (TLS). NAS servers must use HTTPS, and a self-signed certificate is trusted only after you confirm its fingerprint.
  • The NAS password is stored only in the operating system's secure storage.
  • File paths, server addresses and account-like text are removed on the device before error reports and problem reports are sent.
  • The developer runs no servers, so no personal information is collected and stored centrally.
